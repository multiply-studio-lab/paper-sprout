TOPIK Question 54: A 3-Paragraph Plan for the Essay

Question 54 is worth 50 of the 100 writing points: as much as Questions 51–53 combined. You get a short background text and usually three guiding questions, and you must write 600–700 characters without copying the question.

One paragraph per question

Paragraph Job Openers
1 Answer question 1 (background, causes) in your own words 최근 ~(으)면서 ~이/가 늘고 있다. / ~의 원인으로는 ~을/를 들 수 있다.
2 Answer question 2 with two points and an example 첫째, ~. 둘째, ~. / 예를 들어 ~.
3 Answer question 3: your solution or opinion 따라서 ~기 위해서는 ~아/어야 한다. / 나는 ~다고 생각한다.

A 30-minute routine

  1. 5 minutes: plan one or two lines per question: your answer plus one reason or example.
  2. 20 minutes: write three paragraphs. Start each with an indent square.
  3. 5 minutes: check that every sentence ends in -다, then check spelling and length. On a 25-square grid, 600 characters is row 24 and 700 is row 28.

The paper-based writing section is 50 minutes for all four questions, so 30 minutes for Question 54 leaves about 20 for 51–53. Adjust to your own pace.

Do not copy the prompt

The instruction says 문제를 그대로 옮겨 쓰지 마시오. If the prompt says "온라인 수업이 많이 활용되고 있다", swapping one word (많이 → 널리) is still copying. Say the same idea in a new way:

이제 온라인 수업은 초등학생부터 직장인까지 누구나 쉽게 접하는 학습 방법으로 자리 잡았다.

Expressions that hold an essay together

첫째/둘째 · 예를 들어 · ~(으)ㄹ 뿐만 아니라 · 그러나 · ~(으)ㄴ/는 것은 아니다 · 이처럼 · 따라서 · ~기보다 · 나는 ~다고 생각한다 · ~아/어야 할 것이다
