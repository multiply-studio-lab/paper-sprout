TOPIK Questions 51 and 52: Find the Answer in the Clues

Questions 51 and 52 open the TOPIK II writing section. Each one shows a short text with two blanks, marked ㉠ and ㉡, and the instruction is:

※ [51~52] 다음 글의 ㉠과 ㉡에 알맞은 말을 각각 쓰시오. (각 10점)

Each question is worth 10 points, so together they are worth 20 of the 100 writing points. They are short, but they are not free points: the answer has to fit the meaning, the grammar around the blank and the style of the text.

Two kinds of text

Question 51 Question 52
Text Notice, email, message or online post Short explanatory paragraph
Style of your answer Same as the text, usually -습니다 Plain written style, -다

The most common way to lose points is a style mismatch: a -요 ending in Question 52, or a casual ending in a formal notice.

Four clues that point to the answer

  1. Linking words. 그런데, 하지만, 그래서, 따라서, 반면에 and 오히려 tell you whether the blank continues the idea, turns against it or concludes it.
  2. The words touching the blank. A question mark after the blank means you write a question. Words after the blank (for example 알고 싶습니다 or 노력해야 한다) mean your answer must connect to them.
  3. The purpose of the text. In Question 51, an invitation invites, a notice informs and then asks readers to do something, and a lost-item post asks for help.
  4. The sentence that explains. The sentence after the blank often gives a reason (-기 때문이다), which tells you what the blank must say.

A worked example

사람들은 흔히 칭찬은 많이 할수록 좋다고 생각한다. 그러나 모든 칭찬이 아이에게 ( ㉠ ).

  • 흔히 ~ 생각한다 (people usually think) introduces a common belief.
  • 그러나 (however) tells you the blank will push back against it.
  • 모든 (all) is the classic signal for a partial negation.

So ㉠ is 좋은 것은 아니다 (is not always good). The pattern 모든 ~ -(으)ㄴ/는 것은 아니다 appears again and again in Question 52-style texts.

A 10-minute routine

  1. Read the whole text and sum up its purpose in a few words.
  2. Circle the linking word and the words right next to each blank.
  3. Write only the words that complete the sentence, in the text's style.
  4. Read the whole line in your head with your answer in it.
