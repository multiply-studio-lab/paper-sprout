7 Everyday Korean Words for Beginners, with Example Sentences

A word sticks when you meet it in a real sentence, say it out loud and write it once by hand. Here are seven everyday words to start with. Each one has a natural example sentence in polite speech (the -요 / -습니다 style you will use with people you don't know well).

Word Romanization Meaning Example
사과 sagwa apple 저는 아침마다 사과를 먹어요. I eat an apple every morning.
친구 chingu friend 주말에 친구를 만나요. I meet my friend on the weekend.
비싸다 bissada to be expensive 이 가방은 너무 비싸요. This bag is too expensive.
맛있다 masitda to be delicious 이 김치찌개가 정말 맛있어요. This kimchi stew is really delicious.
기다리다 gidarida to wait 지금 버스를 기다리고 있어요. I'm waiting for the bus now.
날씨 nalssi weather 오늘은 날씨가 좋아요. The weather is nice today.
감사하다 gamsahada to be thankful 도와주셔서 감사합니다. Thank you for helping me.

Three small grammar notes

  • 비싸다 → 비싸요. Dictionary forms end in -다. In conversation, drop -다 and add -아요/-어요: 비싸다 → 비싸요, 맛있다 → 맛있어요.
  • 기다리고 있어요. -고 있어요 means "I'm ...ing" right now.
  • 감사합니다 is the safe "thank you" for teachers, shops and strangers. 도와주셔서 means "because you helped (me)".

How to remember them

  1. Listen to the word and the sentence twice.
  2. Say both out loud.
  3. Write the word once and the sentence once by hand. Writing makes you notice every letter, including double consonants like ㅆ in 비싸다.
