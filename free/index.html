How to use them

  1. Start with the Hangul sheet if you can't read Korean yet. Say each sound out loud while you trace.
  2. Once you know the letters, do one puzzle a day. Look up any word you can't read, then write it once by hand.
  3. Check the answer pages last, not first.

Want to go further? The guides on the blog cover stroke order, the two number systems and polite verb endings.
